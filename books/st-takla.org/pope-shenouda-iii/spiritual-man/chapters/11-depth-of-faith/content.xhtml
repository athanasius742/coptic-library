11- The depth of faith

 

The common faith is claimed by all, but not every faithful is deep in it. Peter the Apostle believed for some time. He walked on the water to go to Jesus and when his faith weakened, he fell and the Lord said to him: “O you of little faith why did you doubt?” (Mt 14:31). The deep faith does not doubt or fear but it can move the mountains. The best saying about the deep faith is:

“All things are possible to him who believes ” (Mk 9:23).

Faith which has its power, its victory and its effectiveness to contain the whole life.
