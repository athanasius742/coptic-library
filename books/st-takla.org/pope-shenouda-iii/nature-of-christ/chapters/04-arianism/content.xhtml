A) The Heresy of Arius (Arianism):

Arius denied the Divinity of the Lord Jesus Christ; he considered that Christ was not consubstantial with the Father and that He was created.

The roots of Arianism still exist until this day. Even after being condemned in 325 AD by the Council of Nicea, Arius and his followers caused trouble, dissension and suspicions within the Holy Church.
