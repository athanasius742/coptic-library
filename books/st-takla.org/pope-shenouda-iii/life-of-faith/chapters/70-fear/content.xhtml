6-Fear

Fear weakens the faith and a weak faith leads to fear. Saint Peter, the great apostle, denied Jesus, when he feared. He insulted, cursed and swore that he didn't know the man (Matt 26:74), and thus his faith weakened. Jesus told him before that “I have prayed for you, that your faith should not fail.” (Luke 22:32).

Many lose their faith due to fear, as we have discussed this issue before here on st-takla.org in other sections. That is why Revelation puts the cowards at the top of the list of perishers, and said “But the cowardly, unbelieving, abominable... shall have their part in the lake which burns with fire and brimstone.” (Rev. 21:8).
