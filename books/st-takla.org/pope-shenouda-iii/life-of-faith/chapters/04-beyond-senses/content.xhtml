4- Faith Is Beyond The Level Of Our Senses

 

St-Takla.org Image: The five senses: touch, sight, hearing, taste and smell.

صورة في موقع الأنبا تكلا: الحواس الخمسة: حاسة اللمس - حاسة النظر - حاسة السمع - حاسة التذوق - حاسة الشم.

Faith does not contradict but beyond our senses. Faith is a higher power than our limited senses. Our senses are only capable of detecting physical or materialistic things, but there are other matters, which are beyond the material sense. The senses are also limited in what they can physically detect.

Instruments are often used to sense details and gain information which the senses alone can not accomplish. How much more is it for the non-physical, which the apostle called “invisible things”. Faith is not what is seen by the human eye (2 Cor. 5:7). The soul can not be seen or felt by the senses. The fact that the senses do not feel the soul does not mean that it does not exist but rather, unable to detect it. The senses are limited in scope and the level of the soul.
