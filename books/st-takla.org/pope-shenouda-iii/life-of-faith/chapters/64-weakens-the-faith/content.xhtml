64- What weakens the faith

 

The devil labours continuously with all his efforts to weaken the believers' faith. He and his companions try to deceive even the elect (Matt. 24:24). It does not suffice them only to weaken the faith, but even to turn their victim into an apostate. This is how in the end of days many will abandon the faith “Giving heed to deceiving spirits and doctrines of demons” (1 Tim. 4: 1). How dangerous is the Bible's saying about the Second Coming of Christ “Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will He really find faith on the earth” (Luke 18:8).

What are the demon's tricks to weaken the faith? There are many: some are terrible, and some are calm that nobody can feel.
