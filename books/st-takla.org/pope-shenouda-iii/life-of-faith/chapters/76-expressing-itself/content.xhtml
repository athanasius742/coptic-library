1-Faith Expressing Itself Through Love (Gal. 5:6):

Test your faith in love according to what the apostle explained in (1 Cor. 13) “Love suffers long and is kind; love does not envy; love does not parade itself, is not puffed up; does not behave rudely, does not seek its own, is not provoked, thinks no evil; does not rejoice in iniquity, but rejoices in the truth; bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.” (1 Cor. 13:4-7).

Do you have all these qualities so as to have a sound faith? The apostle said “And though I have all faith, so that I could remove mountains, but have not love, I am nothing.” (1 Cor 13:2). With this love, you can examine your faith... Yet in general, you can examine your faith through your deeds.
