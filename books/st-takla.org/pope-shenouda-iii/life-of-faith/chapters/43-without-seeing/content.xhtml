3. Faith Without Seeing

This faith does not depend on the senses and is consistent with what the Lord said “Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.” (John 20:29). It is not like the scientists who do not believe in anything unless they get it in their laboratories and convince themselves of it with their eyes and instruments, and not like the Sadducees who denied the existence of the angels, the resurrection and the spirits (Acts 23:8), because they have not seen anything from all that...
