[10] How Long Should We Continue Offering This Sacrament?

We should continue until Christ comes back on the Day of the General Resurrection. This is to say for all the days of our lives.

This is a Scriptural teaching, which is not limited to one period of history, nor was it meant only for the apostolic age.
