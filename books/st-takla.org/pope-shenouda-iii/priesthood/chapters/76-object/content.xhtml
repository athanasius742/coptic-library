[25] Some Object That Christ’s Sacrifice can not Be Repeated

Yes we agree that the Sacrifice of Christ is one, but it is a perpetual sacrifice.

Christ wanted it to perpetuate, otherwise why did He say “Do this... until I return”.

By handing the Sacrament to Saint Paul years after His resurrection, He showed His desire for its perpetuity.

Also note:

(Psa 110:4) The LORD has sworn And will not relent, "You are a priest forever According to the order of Melchizedek."
