24- The Clergy Are Selected Men Assigned Special Tasks

 

[17] The Selected Men Of The Lord

We read in the Gospel:

(Luke 16:12-13) "And if you have not been faithful in what is another man's, who will give you what is your own? {13} "No servant can serve two masters; for either he will hate the one and love the other, or else he will be loyal to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and mammon."

(Luke 10:1) After these things the Lord appointed seventy others also, and sent them two by two before His face into every city and place where He Himself was about to go.

From the above one can deduce the following:

1 Priesthood is a divine plan.

2 Priesthood was established by the Lord Himself.

3 He started with the Apostles, assigning them the work, and the place.
