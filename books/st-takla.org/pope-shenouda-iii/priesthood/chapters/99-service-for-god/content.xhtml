Three Basic Services

The priests are Servants of God and Christ. But what is their duty statement as servants?

They serve in all directions, but we will highlight three (3) main areas of service:

(1) The Service of the Altar

(2) The Service of the Scriptures

(3) The Reconciliation Service.
