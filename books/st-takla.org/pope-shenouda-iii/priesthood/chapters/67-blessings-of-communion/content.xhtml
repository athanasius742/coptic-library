[16] The Benefits And Blessings Of Communion.

The benefits and blessings of communion are spiritually superior, which can not be received from ordinary bread. Communion grants Eternal Life, steadfastness in the Lord and Forgiveness of Sins Eternal Life

(John 6:54) "Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.
