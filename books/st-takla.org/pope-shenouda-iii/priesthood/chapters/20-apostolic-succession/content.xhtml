20- The Apostolic succession and the laying on of hands

 

St-Takla.org Image: An icon of Saint Timothy the Bishop.

صورة في موقع الأنبا تكلا: أيقونة القديس تيموثاوس الأسقف.

Saint Paul laid his hands on many, and included Saint Timothy the bishop and Saint Titus the bishop. He said to Saint Timothy:

(2 Tim 1:6) Therefore I remind you to stir up the gift of God which is in you through the laying on of my hands.

It is clear that God’s gift was passed on from Saint Paul to his disciple Timothy.
