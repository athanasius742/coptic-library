The Third Ecumenical Council:  The Council of Ephesus.: Canon IV

Early Church Fathers  Index     

Canon IV.

If any of the clergy should fall away, and publicly or privately presume to maintain the doctrines of Nestorius or Celestius, it is declared just by the holy Synod that these also should be deposed.

Notes.

Ancient Epitome of Canon IV.

If any of the clergy shall consent to Celestine 264 or Nestorius, let them be deposed.

Footnotes

229:264

It should read “Celestius” see Scholion on Canon I.
